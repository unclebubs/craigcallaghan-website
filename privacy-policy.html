Privacy Policy

This website is operated by Craig Callaghan. I am committed to keeping the website simple, respectful and privacy-conscious.

Information I collect

I do not intentionally collect personal data through this website. I do not request names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other personal details as part of routine site use.

I do not process customer accounts, member registrations, contact form submissions, or direct marketing data on this site. There is no personal data collection beyond standard website analytics activity described in the cookie policy.

Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with the site and to monitor general traffic patterns. This is used only for aggregate reporting and website improvement.

Google Analytics does not create a personal profile for individual visitors for the purpose of this website, and I do not use the data for advertising, profiling or unrelated tracking purposes.

Cookies

The only cookie used on this website is the Google Analytics cookie set by that service. No additional cookies are used for advertising, preferences, personalisation, social media tracking, or other data collection.

Third parties

This site does not share personal data with third parties, and it does not sell or rent visitor information. The only third-party service in use is Google Analytics for website statistics.

Your rights

If you have any concerns about privacy, you are welcome to contact me directly. I can explain what information is collected and how it is used.

If you would like to discuss this further, you can email craig.callaghan@gmail.com.
