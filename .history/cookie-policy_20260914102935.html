Cookie Policy

This website uses Google Analytics to help understand how visitors use the site and to improve the experience over time. The only cookie used on this website is the Google Analytics cookie set by that service.

What cookies are used

Google Analytics may set standard analytics cookies such as _ga, _gid, and related session identifiers to measure aggregate traffic and user behaviour. This is the only cookie category used on this website.

I do not use advertising cookies, marketing cookies, social media tracking cookies, or any other third-party tracking tools. I do not place additional cookies for preferences, surveys, remarketing, or personalisation.

Personal data and privacy

I do not intentionally collect personal data through this website. I do not ask for names, email addresses, account information, payment details, or any other personal information as part of normal website use.

The analytics data is used only for aggregate website reporting, such as page views, traffic sources, device type, and general user behaviour patterns. It is not used to identify individuals or build personal profiles.

Managing cookies

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. If you prefer, you can also choose to block Google Analytics in your browser or use browser privacy tools to prevent cookie tracking.
